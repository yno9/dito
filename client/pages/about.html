A tool for digital identity rights

Every identity is yours to dispose of — to use, or to dispense with.

You have the right to be identified: to let one host among others publish a name, a key, a log — verifiably present in the world.

You have the right not to be identified: to leave no trace, to stay anonymous, yet reachable.

To own a digital identity means it is physically in your hands, offline, owing nothing to any network.

To be disposable is not to be disposed of. It is to remain, at every step, the one who decides.
